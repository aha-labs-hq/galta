Galta 0.1.5

  • When an agent adds to the canvas, you watch it draw: each stroke follows its path the way a hand would, and lines, shapes and text appear in their turn. Time Machine plays changes back the same way, going to each one where it happened. Click the canvas to see the finished work at once.
  • Chat in Galta looks at what it draws: it can number what is on the canvas and lay a grid over it, and it checks its own brush work before calling it done. Each turn carries less of your coding setup, so it costs less.
  • Chat in Galta runs on your own Codex, from ChatGPT or Codex for Mac, Homebrew or npm. Without one, Galta downloads it when you ask in Settings, and updating keeps the Codex your Galta already has.
  • Canvases with thousands of brush strokes stay smooth to pan and zoom, and no longer go blank when you zoom in.
  • A large canvas saves each change by itself, so it saves and opens faster and its history takes far less space.
  • Web pages sit under a browser bar, and pages, images and videos can carry a title and a caption.
  • The canvas paper has a quiet grain.
  • Galta for Mac is an eighth of its former size to download, and its disk image opens on a hand-drawn install window.
