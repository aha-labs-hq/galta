Galta 0.1.6

  • Point and talk: Record captures your canvas and pointing, with microphone audio. Stop to review a local draft, then choose Send to ask your agent to work from it. Shift+R starts recording, Enter stops and keeps the draft, and Escape cancels.
  • Recorded speech detects its language automatically. Only Send uploads audio to Cloudflare for transcription; canvas images and pointer observations stay out of the speech service. Hosted speech requires a verified Aha Labs account and is subject to a shared free allowance. Failed or stopped requests keep the original recording available to restore.
  • Chat in Galta now keeps its sign-in and conversations separate from your desktop coding app. Sign in with ChatGPT again inside Galta after updating. Existing canvases and saved transcripts stay available. If an older conversation cannot continue, start a new conversation on the same canvas; your draft is retained. Old native conversation context is not automatically migrated.
  • Answer the agent's questions directly in the input area, including multiple-choice and custom answers, without losing your draft.
  • Image-heavy conversations avoid repeated reconnect attempts, and stopping or recovering an interrupted task preserves its result and draft.
